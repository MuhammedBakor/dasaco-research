DA-SACO NRF Scaling Eligibility

Result: CONDITIONALLY ELIGIBLE WITH GUARDED NF RE-REGISTRATION

Validated:
- NRF scaled from 1 to 2 replicas
- Both replicas became Kubernetes Ready
- Both replicas returned identical discovery results
- Both replicas accessed the shared MongoDB-backed NF profile store
- Service request counts were 22 and 22
- Registration Accepts: 10
- Configuration Update Completes: 10
- Registration Rejects: 0
- NRF returned from 2 replicas to 1 at the Kubernetes level

Observed scale-down failure:
- Direct NRF scale-down removed the shared NF profiles
- MongoDB NfProfile count became zero
- Discovery returned:
  {"validityPeriod":100,"nfInstances":null}

Validated guarded recovery:
- AMF, AUSF, UDM, UDR, and PCF were restarted after NRF scale-down
- The functions registered new NF profiles
- AMF discovery returned a REGISTERED profile
- NRF discovery continuity was restored

Eligibility decision:
- Blind NRF scale-down is not eligible
- NRF scale-out is operational
- NRF scale-down is conditionally eligible only through a specialized
  recovery transaction that forces NF re-registration and verifies
  discovery before declaring recovery complete
