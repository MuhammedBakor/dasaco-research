DA-SACO Open5GLoS Scaling Eligibility

Result: CONDITIONALLY ELIGIBLE WITH CONNECTION-AWARE DRAINING

Validation:
- Open5GLoS scaled from 1 to 2 replicas
- Both replicas became Kubernetes Ready
- Both replicas connected independently to the current AMF
- Kubernetes distributed two SCTP gNB connections across both replicas

Per-replica traffic:
- Replica 1: 1 gNB connection, 10 Initial UE Messages, 10 successful forwards
- Replica 2: 1 gNB connection, 10 Initial UE Messages, 10 successful forwards

Registration result:
- Planned UEs: 20
- Registration Accepts: 20
- Configuration Update Completes: 20
- Registration Rejects: 0
- Broken pipes: 0
- Disconnected gNB removals: 2

State constraint:
- gNB, UE, UE-to-AMF binding, and connection state are local to each
  Open5GLoS replica.
- UEs cannot migrate between Open5GLoS replicas during an active SCTP
  association.
- Kubernetes distributes complete SCTP connections, not individual UEs
  inside an existing connection.

Recovery decision:
- Blind scale-down while a target replica owns active gNB connections
  is unsafe.
- Open5GLoS scale-down is eligible only after connection-aware draining:
  stop new connections to the selected replica, wait until active gNB
  count reaches zero, and then reduce the Deployment replica count.

Conclusion:
Open5GLoS is eligible for guarded horizontal scale-out and
connection-aware scale-down. Newly created replicas provided useful
capacity when new gNB SCTP associations were established.
